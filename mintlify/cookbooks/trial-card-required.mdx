In a free trial, users begin with the full, paid version of the product automatically enabled. 

After a set period they must either subscribe or the premium features are withdrawn and they drop back to a limited free tier. 

This is sometimes called a "reverse trial". Companies that use this model include Greptile, Granola and Miro.

## Configure Pricing

Configure the type of trial you want to use in the Plan Settings section.

### Paid plan trial: card required

Set up a trial where users must provide payment information upfront. This will automatically transition to a paid plan subscription when the trial ends.

### Adding a trial fee (optional)

Charge a small upfront fee for trial access, often used to qualify leads and reduce trial abuse while maintaining low barrier to entry.

You can create an add-on plan, with a one-time price. We will attach the plan with a free trial, and the trial-fee add on at the same time.
